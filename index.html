Retune

Change the tempo and pitch of an audio file, then arrange it

Retune is a browser tool for producers, DJs and editors. Drop a track, read its detected BPM, type the BPM or the pitch you want, layer clips on lanes, and export a WAV. Everything runs inside this page: no upload, no account, no network request.

Or drop files anywhere on this window, or paste them from the clipboard.

Detected BPM per file, with a manual override and tap tempo.

Tempo and pitch move independently, or together in linked mode.

Export the whole project, the selection range, or one file per lane.
